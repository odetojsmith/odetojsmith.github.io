I am an Assistant Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). Prior to joining CUHK-Shenzhen, I worked in industry for over two years, with hands-on experience in building hardware data models and deploying design-automation solutions in real-world settings.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from EPFL, Switzerland, co-advised by Prof. Giancarlo Ferrari-Trecate and Prof. Maryam Kamgarpour. Prior to that, I obtained my M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology from ETH Zürich.

My research sits at the intersection of Control Theory, Mathematical Optimization, and Machine Learning, with an emphasis on developing safe, sample-efficient algorithms for real-world cyber-physical and automated engineering systems.

Research Directions

In control and hardware optimization, model-based methods rely heavily on manual expertise and struggle in complex scenarios, while learning-based methods demand massive data and are difficult to trust. We therefore pursue the synergy of these two paradigms, striving to create control and optimization methods that are efficient and robust.

Flexible & Resilient Optimization

A Flexible and Resilient Optimization Platform via Agentic AI

We are building an optimization platform that unifies control and hardware design under a single architecture:

  1. Agentic problem abstraction. Agentic AI translates multimodal engineering inputs — images, audio, sensor signals — into well-posed mathematical formulations.
  2. Mixed-integer, unified architecture. Hybrid discrete-continuous decisions are supported natively, with diverse problem settings served without architectural changes.
  3. Sample-efficient Bayesian optimization. Classical-optimization acceleration (trust regions, structured surrogates) turns previously intractable problems into solvable ones.
Trustworthy Learning + MPC

Trustworthy Learning-Augmented MPC for Embodied Intelligence

We combine learning-based methods with model-based MPC for efficient, interpretable, and trustworthy control of embodied systems:

  1. Amortizing MPC with imitation learning. Learned policies inherit the interpretable MPC structure, replacing intractable real-time optimization with a single forward pass.
  2. Safe, model-free hyperparameter tuning. Bayesian optimization with sampling safety guarantees reconciles conflicting design objectives.
  3. Bridging the sim-to-real gap. Enabling reliable transfer of MPC-based controllers to physical robots.
Hardware-Control Co-Design

Hardware-Control Co-Design via Bi-Level Bayesian Optimization

Hardware and control are still designed in isolation, capping system performance. We co-optimize them as one coupled problem via bi-level Bayesian optimization:

  1. Bi-level co-optimization. An outer BO loop searches hardware designs; the embedded inner loop of numerical optimization synthesizes the matching controller.
  2. Tractable joint search. Meta-learned warm starts let the inner loop converge within a few iterations.
  3. High-impact applications. Energy reduction in intelligent driving, and robot morphology-control co-design for agile, efficient robots.
