I am an Assistant Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). Prior to joining CUHK-Shenzhen, I worked in industry for over two years, with hands-on experience in building hardware data models and deploying design-automation solutions in real-world settings.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from EPF Lausanne (EPFL), Switzerland, co-advised by Prof. Giancarlo Ferrari-Trecate and Prof. Maryam Kamgarpour. Prior to that, I obtained my M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology from ETH Zürich.

My research sits at the intersection of Control Theory, Mathematical Optimization, and Machine Learning, with an emphasis on developing safe, sample-efficient algorithms for real-world cyber-physical and automated engineering systems.

Research Directions

Flexible & Resilient Optimization

A Flexible and Resilient Optimization Platform via Agentic AI

We are building a next-generation optimization platform that unifies control design and hardware design under a single mathematical architecture, with three key thrusts:

  1. Agentic problem abstraction. Agentic AI automatically translates engineering problems from control and hardware design into well-posed mathematical formulations, seamlessly bridging domain practice and optimization.
  2. Mixed-integer, unified architecture. The platform natively supports hybrid discrete-continuous decision variables and accommodates diverse problem settings without altering the underlying problem architecture.
  3. Sample-efficient Bayesian optimization. By integrating acceleration schemes, trust regions, and structured surrogates from classical optimization, we dramatically improve sample efficiency — turning previously intractable problems into solvable ones.
Trustworthy Learning + MPC

Trustworthy Learning-Augmented MPC for Embodied Intelligence

We pursue trustworthy control for embodied intelligence by combining learning-based methods with model-based MPC, where RL and Bayesian optimization serve to accelerate, tune, and safely deploy MPC controllers:

  1. Amortizing MPC with imitation learning. We train policies that imitate MPC while inheriting its interpretable architecture, replacing computationally intractable real-time optimization with a single forward pass.
  2. Safe, model-free tuning of MPC hyperparameters. Bayesian optimization with sampling-based safety guarantees adjusts MPC hyperparameters to reconcile conflicting design objectives, without relying on an explicit model.
  3. Bridging the sim-to-real gap. The same learning-plus-optimization machinery enables reliable transfer of MPC-based controllers from simulation to physical robot deployment.
Hardware-Control Co-Design

Hardware-Control Co-Design via Bi-Level Bayesian Optimization

Today's engineering paradigm designs hardware and control in isolation — a decoupling that prevents systems from reaching their full potential. We treat physical design and control as a single coupled optimization problem, solved through bi-level Bayesian optimization with numerical optimization embedded in the inner loop:

  1. Bi-level co-optimization. An outer Bayesian optimization loop searches over hardware designs, while the embedded inner loop synthesizes the matching control policy, jointly optimizing coupled variables that sequential pipelines cannot handle.
  2. Tractable joint search. Meta-learned warm starts and surrogate models allow the inner loop to converge within a few iterations, making the co-design of "body" and "brain" computationally feasible.
  3. High-impact applications. Representative scenarios include energy consumption reduction in intelligent driving — co-optimizing vehicle/powertrain design with driving policies — and robot morphology-control co-design for agile, energy-efficient manipulation and locomotion.
