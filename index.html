I am an Assistant Professor at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). Previously, I was a Senior Algorithm Engineer at Huawei Technologies 2012 Labs.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from EPF Lausanne (EPFL), Switzerland, co-advised by Prof. Giancarlo Ferrari-Trecate and Prof. Maryam Kamgarpour. Prior to that, I obtained my M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering and Information Technology from ETH Zürich, and dual B.Sc. degrees in Electrical Engineering and Applied Mathematics from Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT).

My research sits at the intersection of Control Theory, Machine Learning, and Mathematical Optimization, with an emphasis on developing safe, sample-efficient algorithms for real-world cyber-physical and automated engineering systems.

Research Directions

Safe & Sample-Efficient BO

Safe and Efficient AI-driven Black-Box Optimization

Optimization frameworks for complex systems without analytical objective functions or constraint formulations. We focus on guaranteeing strict constraint satisfaction during exploration (safety) while dramatically improving sample efficiency. Recent efforts integrate Bayesian Optimization (BO) with Reinforcement Learning and Generative Foundation Models (e.g., Diffusion Models for BO) to enable rapid, reliable deployment on real-world systems and lifelong learning tasks.

Data-Driven Robust Control

Data-Driven Robust Control & RL-MPC

Direct data-driven receding-horizon control methodologies without explicit system identification bottlenecks. By conducting perturbation analysis on behavioral system models (e.g., Willems' fundamental lemma) and synthesizing predictive control with policy optimization, we develop safe controllers that maintain robustness against system uncertainties, non-Gaussian disturbances, and non-stationary dynamic environments.

Hardware-Algorithm Co-Design

Expertise-Driven Hardware Design Automation & Co-Synthesis

Developing automated hardware design frameworks based on multi-modal black-box optimization. We incorporate domain-specific human expertise and multi-modal models to prune suboptimal design subspaces. Furthermore, we explore the co-synthesis of hardware architectures and control algorithms, jointly optimizing physical parameters and feedback policies using proxy models and warm-start acceleration.
